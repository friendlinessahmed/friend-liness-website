About Friend Liness

Reliable Marine Supply. Responsive Service. Professional Delivery.

FRIEND LINESS is a Karachi-based ship chandler and marine supply company supporting vessels calling Karachi Port, Port Qasim and anchorage. We focus on dependable, cost-effective vessel supply services, including fresh, frozen and dry provisions together with selected vessel consumables.

Our aim is simple: understand the requisition, quote competitively, coordinate delivery and support the vessel on time.
